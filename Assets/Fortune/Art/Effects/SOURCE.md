# Particle textures

Selected original textures from [Particle Pack](https://kenney.nl/assets/particle-pack) by Kenney, version 1.1 as identified in the included license.

- `smoke_01.png`, `smoke_04.png`: textured smoke clouds.
- `fire_01.png`: blast core.
- `scorch_01.png`: impact burst.
- `trace_01.png`: ember streak.

All five files are the unmodified 512 × 512 transparent PNG originals from the pack’s `PNG (Transparent)` folder. The game applies colour and motion at runtime.
